DELIVERY POLICY

1. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.
2. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.
3. Standard delivery costs £3.99.
4. Express delivery costs £7.99.
5. Free standard delivery is available for orders above £50.
6. Customers receive a tracking link by email after dispatch.
7. Delivery is available Monday to Saturday.
8. If an order is delayed, customers should contact customer support with their order number.
