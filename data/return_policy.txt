RETURN POLICY

1. Customers can return products within 30 days of delivery.
2. Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
3. Customers must provide their order number when requesting a return.
4. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product is inspected.
5. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the product was damaged or incorrect.
6. Sale items are eligible for return unless marked as final sale.
7. Customers can request a return by contacting customer support.
