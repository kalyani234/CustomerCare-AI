COMPANY FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. What are your customer support hours?
Our customer support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

2. How can I contact customer support?
You can contact our support team through email or the customer support chat.

3. Do you deliver internationally?
Currently, we deliver only within the United Kingdom.

4. Can I change my order after placing it?
You can request an order change before the order has been dispatched.

5. How can I track my order?
You can track your order using the tracking link sent to your registered email address.

6. Do I need an account to place an order?
No. Customers can place orders as guests without creating an account.
